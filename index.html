Light is my favourite subject

Night, fog and street photography by Rhethim

What my camera is good at

Reading light

Streetlamps, flames, fog, the first sun through the trees. My best frames let the light do most of the work.

Finding texture

Raw concrete, peeling paint, wet stone floors. Old walls and tired buildings get as much of my attention as faces.

Waiting for the moment

A river aarti at dusk, an empty alley at night, a walkway over loud water. I wait for the frame, then press the shutter.
